Questions

Before you join

Do I have to use my real name?

No. You’ll make a new TikTok account, and it doesn’t have to be your real name. You do have to show your face.

What do I need?

Your phone, a tripod, and somewhere with good daylight. Extra lighting is optional. Daylight is best.

I’m scared of being seen. Is this for me?

Yes. That’s exactly what the energy work is for. We start gently, and your first video only goes to the group.

Will I go viral or get followers?

I can’t promise followers, sales, or virality, and nobody honest can. What I promise is the energy work, the skills, and the reps: 30 videos on your account over six weeks, with me beside you.

Where does the group happen?

Lessons and replays live in a private classroom only for Show Thyself members. Daily chat, sharing videos, and following each other happens in a private Telegram group. Energy Academy is included while you’re enrolled.

How much time does it take?

One live workshop a week (60–90 minutes, recorded), a few minutes of energy practice a day, and five short videos a week.
