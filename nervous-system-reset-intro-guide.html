Introduction
Nervous system regulation starts with feeling safe in your body and your energy field. This guide provides foundational practices to help you establish safety, ground your body, and begin your journey toward nervous system regulation.

Stage 1: Establishing Your Energy Field

Understanding Your Energy Field

Your energy field is your first line of defense and your primary tool for feeling safe. When your field is strong and clear, your nervous system can relax.

Stop Energetic Leaks

Cut off energy to people, places, or things that make you feel:

  • Ungrounded
  • Unsafe
  • Depleted after being with them
  • Just plain icky

How to tell if you have an energy leak: If you feel depleted after being with someone or doing something, that's an energy leak. Cut it off as much as physically possible.

Once you're solid and grounded, you may be able to reintroduce these people, but you probably won't want to if they're not doing the work on their end.

Get Out in Nature

Nature stabilizes and grounds your nervous system. This is what she does. Get fresh air and the energy from nature as often as possible.

Stage 3: Body Work

Once your energy field is established, your body can be grounded through somatic healing practices.

Specific energetic methods are provided in the full Nervous System Sanctuary course.

Physical Foundation

  • Drink lots of water - Most people live in low-grade panic because they're dehydrated. Have a full water bottle every hour if needed.
  • Eat well - Nourish your body with real food
  • Exercise - Walk in the park, do yoga at home, Pilates, treadmill, or any movement. This helps your brain chemicals and is only possible after you've made space and feel safe to do it.

Stage 4: Affirmations & Programming

Programming your output is how you get control of your reality. This is repeating an affirmation for 10 minutes straight at least once a day.

How to Practice

  1. Choose an affirmation that moves you forward
  2. Repeat it for 10 minutes straight, at least once a day
  3. Commit to this for 2 weeks

Example affirmations:

  • "I am safe in my body"
  • "Everything always works out for me"

Mental discipline is required. Some people don't want to do this because they like their misery. If you don't like your misery and want to change it, commit 10 minutes a day for two weeks.
