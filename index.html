The beauty routine

You are 31, oily, large-pored, and sensitive, with a clear face and trouble on the chest, back, and in rashes. You just changed countries. Heat, sweat, mineral-heavy water, sunscreen, and conditioner are enough to explain a lot of that. The healthiest routine is short, fragrance-free, and repeated. Organic is optional. Perfume is the thing to retire, including essential oils. Snail mucin stays off the shelf.

Calm, then protect

1
Cleanse. Centella wash on damp skin. In the shower, wash your back after conditioner, with hair clipped up.
2
Mist. Hypochlorous acid spray, let it dry. This replaces rose water. It is water, salt, and an acid your skin already understands. Kind to rashes and to sweat.
3

4

5
Light gel cream if the skin feels tight. Oily dehydrated skin still needs water. It does not need a rich butter.
6
SPF 50. The real anti-aging step, especially in Mexico. Face, neck, and chest if the chest sees sun. Reapply if you are outside.

Chest, back, rashes

A four-week body protocol

  • Shower soon after sweat. A KDF and carbon shower filter is the highest-leverage thing you can buy in a new water supply.
  • Four nights a week: PanOxyl 4% benzoyl peroxide wash on the chest and back. Leave it on for two minutes. Rinse. It bleaches towels, so keep a white one for this.
  • Other nights: a fragrance-free body wash. On two of those nights, a thin layer of 2% salicylic acid on the chest and back, as long as the skin is not stinging.
  • Two mornings a week: ketoconazole 1% shampoo, the kind sold as Nizoral, as a three-minute wash on the chest and back. Heat and conditioner often feed this pattern. If the bumps are itchy and even, this is the step that tells you.
  • Condition the ends, clip the hair up, then wash the back. Sleep in a clean shirt.
  • Fragrance-free detergent. Skip fabric softener.
  • Moisturize the body only where it feels tight, with a light fragrance-free lotion. Keep Cosmetic Lad, castor oil, and face oils off the chest.
  • Give this a month. If a rash spreads, weeps, or comes with swelling, see a dermatologist. A new climate is a good reason to get eyes on it, and this page is a care plan, not a diagnosis.

Hair

Oily scalp and bleached, tired ends are two different jobs. Wash the scalp often. Repair only the lengths. Anything creamy that rinses down your back is part of the chest and back acne, so the fragrance-free shampoo is a skin product as much as a hair product.

Scalp · often

Clean at the root

Wash every day or every other day in this heat. Shampoo on the scalp only, with the pads of the fingers. SEEN Fragrance Free is the one to buy: made so the residue does not clog skin, color-safe, no masking scent. Vanicream Free & Clear is the plainer, cheaper version.

Once a week, a chelating wash such as Malibu C Hard Water Wellness, scalp only. Mineral water makes blonde look dull and leaves the scalp feeling coated.

Ends · twice a week

K18, then a trim

K18 Leave-In Molecular Repair Mask on towel-dry mid-lengths and ends. Leave it four minutes. Do not rinse. Skip conditioner that night. This is the luxury step that matches bleach damage.

On other days, conditioner from the ears down, never the scalp. Two drops of squalane, The Ordinary or Biossance, on the last few inches once the hair is dry. Castor oil on the hair will travel onto your back while you sleep.

Cut the snapped ends. A bond mask can strengthen what is still there. It cannot rejoin a fiber that has already broken.

If you want it Korean

Ends only, scent checked

A lot of Korean hair oils and ampoules are perfumed, and the perfume rinses onto the same skin that is rashy. Use a mask like Unove Damage Treatment on the lengths, in the shower, and rinse it before you wash your back.

If the scent is strong, let it go. The healthier Korean energy in this routine is on your face: centella, Pyunkang Yul, and the Skin1004 sun serum. Your hair gets quieter products, on purpose.

Makeup

Skin and hair come first, so makeup stays thin, fragrance-free, and fully removed at night. Large pores look softer with a serum tint and a little powder on the T-zone than with a heavy pore primer full of scent. On camera, blur the center of the face and leave the skin alive.
