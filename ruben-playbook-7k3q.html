45 minutes

The call, step by step

They talk 70% of the time. You listen for pain, cost, and the dream. Then you give one clear recommendation.

  1. Open and set the frame 2 min

    Thanks for making the time. Here's how this works: I'll ask about where you are and where you want to be. If one of Mariah's offerings is a fit, I'll tell you exactly which one and how to start. If it's not, I'll tell you that too. Sound good?

  2. Discovery: current situation 5 min

    I read your application. You said [their answer]. Walk me through what's going on right now.

    Follow-ups: "Tell me more about that." · "What does that look like day to day?"

  3. Discovery: the cost 5 min

    How long has it been like this? What has it cost you? Money, relationships, sleep, how you feel about yourself?
    What have you already tried? What happened?

  4. Discovery: the dream 5 min

    If we fast-forward six months and this worked, what does your life look like?
    Why now? Why not keep going the way it is?

  5. Reflect the gap 3 min

    So right now it's [current], you want [dream], and the thing in the way is [block]. Did I get that right?

    Wait for a clear yes. If they correct you, reflect again.

  6. Ask permission, then prescribe one offering 5 min

    Can I tell you what I think would fit you best?
    Based on everything you shared, the work that fits you is [offering]. Here's what that looks like for you… [connect each part to their pain and their dream, in their words].

    One recommendation only. Don't list options.

  7. Check fit before price 2 min

    How does that feel to you? Is that the kind of support you've been looking for?

  8. Price and close 5 min

    For High Ritual it's $5,555 paid in full, or three payments of $1,997. Paying in full saves you a little. Mariah only holds four seats, and [X] are open right now. Which works better for you, paying in full or the three payments?

    Then stop talking. Let them answer.

  9. Take payment on the call: send the invoice 3 min

    Confirm their email address out loud, then send the PayPal invoice (steps in Taking payment):

    Perfect. I'm sending your invoice to [email] right now. It has Mariah's terms on it: no refunds unless Mariah feels she's not the right fit, and results aren't guaranteed, because this is spiritual work. You can pay by card or PayPal, you don't need an account. Let me know when you see it.

    Stay on the call until the invoice shows Paid. Then:

    It's through. Welcome, I'm really happy for you. Mariah will reach out within 24 hours with your next steps.

Handle with care

Objections

Get curious, not defensive. Isolate the real reason, answer it, then ask again.

"I need to think about it."

Totally fair. What specifically do you want to think through? Is it the fit, the timing, or the investment?

Then handle whichever one they name.

"I need to talk to my partner."

Makes sense. What do you think they'll ask? Would it help to book 15 minutes with both of you tomorrow?

"It's a lot of money."

It is an investment. You told me this has been going on for [time] and it's cost you [their words]. What would it be worth to you to have that change?

If it's truly the money: offer the payment plan first, then step down one level. Never two.

"Does it really work?"

I can't promise you an outcome, and neither will Mariah. What she guarantees is her full work and her care. She did this for herself first: she went from a 9–5 to living her dream life in Mexico in under a year.

Point to her story and testimonials in Proof.

"I've tried healers before and nothing changed."

What was missing last time? … Most people get either a reading or a session, then they're on their own. With Mariah you get the casting and the guidance together, and she stays with you.

"Can I just start with something smaller?"

You can. Based on what you told me, the smaller option would be [one step down]. It won't cover [the bigger part of their pain], but it's a real start. Want to go with that?

"Now isn't the right time."

What would need to be different for it to be the right time? … And if nothing changes, where are you in six months?

"Can I talk to Mariah first?"

Your private work is directly with Mariah once you start. If you'd like to sit with her first, the 90-minute consult is exactly that.
